TITLE: REZE
ROLE: Soviet Assassin / Bomb Hybrid
DEVIL / CONTRACT: The Bomb Devil
QUOTE: "Tell me, Denji... would you rather be a country mouse or a city mouse?"
BIO: Behind the warm laughter and innocent charm of a part-time cafe girl lies a lethal Soviet weapon engineered from childhood to steal the Chainsaw's heart. By pulling the grenade pin on her neck, Reze detonates into the devastating Bomb Devil hybrid, turning every limb into high-ordnance explosives. Caught between her brutal conditioning as a state assassin and a fleeting dream of running away with Denji, her brief midnight romance in an empty schoolhouse remains an unforgettable tragedy of sparks and rain.

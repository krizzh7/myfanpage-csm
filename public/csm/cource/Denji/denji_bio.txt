TITLE: DENJI
ROLE: Public Safety Devil Hunter / Chainsaw Hybrid
DEVIL / CONTRACT: Pochita (The Chainsaw Devil)
QUOTE: "Show me your dreams, Denji."
BIO: Born into crushing Yakuza debt and robbed of every basic human comfort, Denji survived by hunting devils alongside Pochita, a wounded Chainsaw Devil. When the Yakuza butchered him, Pochita fused with Denji's chest, becoming his heart and awakening the immortal, revving berserker known as Chainsaw Man. Fiercely unpredictable yet disarmingly honest, Denji fights not for grand heroic ideals, but for toast with jam, human warmth, and someone who loves him for Denji rather than the devil beating inside his chest.

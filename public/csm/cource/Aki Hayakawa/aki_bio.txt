TITLE: AKI HAYAKAWA
ROLE: Public Safety Devil Hunter (Special Division 4)
DEVIL / CONTRACT: Curse Devil, Future Devil, Fox Devil
QUOTE: "I want to kill the Gun Devil. That is all that keeps me going."
BIO: Driven by cold, singular vengeance after the Gun Devil wiped out his family in a matter of seconds, Aki Hayakawa serves as the stoic anchor of Special Division 4. Beneath his rigid discipline and willingness to shave years off his own lifespan for lethal devil contracts lies a deeply protective older brother figure. Over time, the chaotic presence of Denji and Power slowly softens his obsession with revenge, replacing his death wish with the fragile warmth of a makeshift family he would give anything to protect.

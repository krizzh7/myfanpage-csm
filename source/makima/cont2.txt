Primordial Domination
As the embodiment of humanity's primordial fear of domination, Makima is the ancient Control Devil. Her terrifying power allows her to manipulate the minds, bodies, and wills of any entity she considers inherently inferior to herself. She can crush targets from hundreds of miles away through ritual sacrifice, borrow the hearing of lower animals across Tokyo, and force fallen devil hunters into perpetual servitude, bending reality itself to ensure her commands are obeyed without question.

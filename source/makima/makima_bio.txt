TITLE: MAKIMA
ROLE: Chief Cabinet Secretary's Personal Devil Hunter
DEVIL / CONTRACT: The Control Devil (Conquest)
QUOTE: "A corpse is talking."
BIO: Soft-spoken, hypnotic, and effortlessly commanding, Makima orchestrates Public Safety's Special Division 4 with a gentle smile and an invisible iron leash. As the embodiment of humanity's primal fear of control and domination, she views humans much like beloved pets—capable of crushing targets from miles away or compelling absolute obedience with a single word. Every kindness, tragedy, and attachment in Denji's new life is meticulously engineered by her hand to shatter his spirit and awaken the true Hero of Hell.

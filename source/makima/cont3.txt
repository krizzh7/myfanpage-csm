The Lonely Tyrant
Despite her chilling ruthlessness and lack of empathy for individual human lives, Makima’s ultimate goal is surprisingly paradoxical: she desires to use Chainsaw Man’s reality-erasing jaw to eliminate death, war, and hunger, crafting a frictionless utopia. Yet, beneath this grandiose ambition hides a profoundly lonely entity incapable of forming genuine relationships. Because her nature demands subjugation, she can never experience true equality, leaving her forever isolated from the very warmth and connection she desperately seeks.

Hubris & Erasure
Makima's supreme arrogance in her own invulnerability ultimately proved to be her downfall. Blinded by her obsessive idolization of Chainsaw Man, she failed to perceive the mortal boy holding the weapon, allowing Denji to deliver a fatal strike using a chainsaw forged from blood. Consumed entirely so her regenerative contracts with Japan's Prime Minister could be bypassed, Makima left behind a world forever altered, reincarnating as an innocent child stripped of her past machinations.

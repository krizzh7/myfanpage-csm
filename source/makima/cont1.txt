The Pristine Facade
Radiating an aura of quiet authority and pristine elegance, Makima stands at the apex of Public Safety's Special Division 4. Her hypnotic yellow ringed eyes and polite, soothing cadence command absolute loyalty from subordinates and government officials alike. Behind her polished veneer lies a calculated strategist who maneuvers human lives and devil threats like pieces on a chessboard, orchestrating cataclysmic conflicts from the shadows while projecting the calming persona of humanity's most benevolent protector.

The Cat and Compassion
Power's journey toward genuine emotion began not through human interaction, but through her unexpected bond with an abandoned cat named Meowy. Initially nursing the animal to fatten it for consumption, she found herself overcome by protective affection, discovering empathy for the first time in her violent existence. When the Bat Devil took Meowy hostage, Power risked everything to secure the cat's safety, proving that beneath her monstrous exterior beat a heart capable of fierce, selfless devotion.

Chaotic Siblinghood
Placed under Aki Hayakawa's supervision alongside Denji, Power blossomed into an indispensable, chaotic pillar of their makeshift household. Her constant antics, food theft, and bizarre tantrums initially drove her housemates to distraction, but the shared trauma of devil hunting forged an unbreakable kinship between them. She became Denji’s blood-sworn sister and partner in crime, leaning on him during her paralyzing bouts of post-traumatic terror following their brush with the Darkness Devil.

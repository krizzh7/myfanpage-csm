Defying Domination
Struck down by Makima's cold betrayal, Power accomplished the impossible by reviving from the remnants of blood Denji had ingested. Defying the overwhelming command of the Control Devil through sheer willpower, she snatched Denji from the jaws of death, shielding him with her own life. In her final moments, she forged an eternal contract with Denji, gifting him her blood and extracting a tearful promise: that he find and befriend the reincarnated Blood Devil once again.

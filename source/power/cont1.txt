The Feral Fiend
Possessing the body of a horned young woman, Power burst into Public Safety as the feral, arrogant Blood Fiend. Boastful, narcissistic, and notorious for fabricating outlandish lies to escape accountability, she views humans as fragile, inferior meatbags. Beneath her unkempt hair, refusal to bathe, and manic cackling lies a wild creature operating purely on primal instincts, whose sharp crimson horns grow larger whenever she consumes an excessive amount of devil blood.

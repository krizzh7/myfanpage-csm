Sanguine Arsenal
In battle, Power is a whirling engine of destruction, manipulating her own blood to construct an arsenal of lethal crimson weaponry. She can solidify blood into colossal hammers that shatter concrete, summon a forest of piercing spears, or manifest thousands of floating daggers to rain down upon her adversaries. Her erratic, berserk fighting style makes her exceptionally dangerous, though her natural cowardice often prompts her to flee the battlefield whenever a foe appears overwhelmingly superior.

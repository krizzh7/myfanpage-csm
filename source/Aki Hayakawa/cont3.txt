Reluctant Guardian
Despite his aloof exterior and strict adherence to workplace regulations, Aki harbors an innate, self-sacrificing compassion. Forced by Makima to house and supervise the chaotic duo of Denji and Power, he quickly shifts from an exasperated caretaker into an indispensable older brother figure. He cooks their meals, cleans up their relentless messes, and quietly pays for their reckless behavior, gradually realizing that their chaotic apartment has become the only real home he has left.

Supernatural Pacts
Aki's combat prowess relies on extreme tactical discipline and high-risk supernatural pacts. Initially summoning the monstrous head of the Fox Devil with a calm whisper of "Kon," he later wields the cursed nail-blade of the Curse Devil to inflict fatal damage at the price of his own vitality. When stripped of these powers, he strikes a haunting contract with the Future Devil, granting him the precognitive ability to glimpse seconds ahead while cursed to witness his worst tragedy.

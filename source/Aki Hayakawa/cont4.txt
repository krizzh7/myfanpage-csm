Relinquishing Revenge
As his bond with Denji and Power deepens, Aki's thirst for vengeance begins to fracture. Terrified of burying another comrade, he seeks to withdraw from the impending Gun Devil assault, choosing the survival of his newfound family over his lifelong vendetta. This tragic vulnerability proves fatal. Manipulated by Makima's inescapable machinations, Aki's desperate attempt to save his friends ultimately delivers him into the very nightmare he fought to avoid, culminating in his horrifying transformation into the Gun Fiend.

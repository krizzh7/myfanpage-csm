Origins & Loss
Driven by a singular obsession, Aki Hayakawa entered Public Safety after witnessing his entire family obliterated in seconds by the Gun Devil. That cold winter morning stripped away his childhood, leaving behind an unyielding resolve to avenge their deaths at any cost. He approaches devil hunting not as a career, but as an inevitable countdown to his own demise, willingly trading away years of his lifespan to forge lethal contracts capable of annihilating every devil in his path.

Found Kinship
Within Special Division 4, Himeno served as an essential social adhesive, organizing drinking parties and fostering camaraderie among the fractured misfits of the bureau. Her messy, affectionate personality offered Denji his first genuine taste of unscripted adult socialization—even when it dissolved into comical disasters and drunken promises. She viewed these chaotic young recruits not as disposable pawns, but as fragile human beings who deserved a chance to live, laugh, and find happiness beyond devil warfare

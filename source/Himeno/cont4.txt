Special space for Denji in her heart
Himeno and Denji shared a chaotic yet deeply genuine camaraderie born of mutual loneliness. After a disastrous drunken kiss, their dynamic evolved from awkward senior-junior friction into an earnest alliance: Himeno promised to help Denji woo Makima in exchange for helping her win Aki’s heart. Beyond their pact, Himeno treated Denji with rare warmth, seeing him as a human rather than a devil weapon. Her tragic sacrifice profoundly shook Denji, forcing him to confront his own capacity for human grief.

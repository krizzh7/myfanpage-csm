The Ghostly Arm
Himeno’s supernatural contract with the Ghost Devil is a haunting reflection of her sacrificial nature. By surrendering her right eye, she gained permanent command over the Ghost Devil’s invisible, intangible right arm. In combat, she uses this spectral limb to throw enemies off balance, manipulate unseen weapons, and retrieve injured teammates from deadly crossfire. It is an understated yet versatile asset that allows her to control the battlefield while keeping herself safely behind the frontlines.

The Melancholy Veteran
With a black eyepatch concealing the empty socket where her right eye once was, Himeno stands as an experienced veteran in Public Safety’s meat grinder. Having survived long enough to bury five successive partners, she uses cynical humor, endless cigarettes, and heavy drinking to numb the crushing survivor’s guilt that haunts her every waking hour. Behind her cheerful, flirtatious demeanor lies a deeply wounded warrior exhausted by the ceaseless cycle of mourning comrades she could not save.

Squalor & The Saw-Heart
Crushed beneath the suffocating weight of his deceased father's Yakuza debts, Denji grew up in squalor, selling his own organs just to survive another day. His only solace was Pochita, an injured canine devil with a chainsaw protruding from his snout. When their ruthless mob masters betrayed and butchered them, Pochita fused with Denji’s dying heart, granting him a second chance at life and transforming him into a revving, immortal hybrid capable of eviscerating any fiend in his path.

Domestic Brotherhood
Recruited into Public Safety under Makima's enigmatic supervision, Denji finds himself thrust into a domestic life he never imagined possible. Sharing an apartment with the fastidious Aki and the manic Blood Fiend Power, he gradually learns what it means to care for others. The daily routine of shared meals, petty bickering, and joint combat operations transforms these three broken outcasts into an inseparable surrogate family, awakening within Denji a profound capacity for unconditional love and loyalty.

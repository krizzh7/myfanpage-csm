Grounded Desires
Unlike conventional protagonists driven by justice or lofty ambitions, Denji's motivations are grounded in raw, fundamental human needs. He fights tooth and nail for toast with jam, an unmolested night of sleep, and a warm bath. This earthy simplicity confounds both allies and eldritch horrors alike. Because his goals are rooted in basic survival rather than fragile moral codes, Denji possesses an unpredictable, berserk resilience in combat, outlasting ancient devils through sheer stubborn absurdity and suicidal grit.

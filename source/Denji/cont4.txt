The Burden of Freedom
Rising from the ashes of betrayal, Denji discovers a poignant, unorthodox method to defeat the immortal Control Devil: an act of total consumption executed out of enduring love. Stepping into the burden of adulthood, he takes custody of the reincarnated Control Devil, Nayuta, and continues his high school education. Balancing part-time devil hunting with the quiet responsibilities of big brotherhood, Denji remains Chainsaw Man—an immortal chainsaw warrior striving perpetually for authentic, uncorrupted human connection.

Orbiting Denji
Asa’s mission to craft a weapon capable of killing Chainsaw Man leads her directly into the orbit of Denji, unaware that the goofy high schooler sitting next to her is the hero she seeks. Their awkward interactions, disastrous aquarium dates, and shared experiences of profound loneliness forge an unexpected, clumsy intimacy. While Yoru pushes to weaponize Denji, Asa’s conscience constantly hesitates, as she recognizes in him a kindred spirit who is just as starved for affection as she is

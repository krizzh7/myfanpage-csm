Defying Fate
As society fractures under the weight of cults and devil worship, Asa evolves from a passive victim of fate into a defiant survivor. Navigating media sensationalism, apocalyptic devil hunters, and Yoru’s escalating bloodlust, she refuses to remain the meek girl who cowered in fear. By weaponizing her guilt and asserting her own desires, Asa charts a messy, chaotic path toward self-acceptance, determined to survive on her own terms no matter what apocalyptic disasters loom ahead.

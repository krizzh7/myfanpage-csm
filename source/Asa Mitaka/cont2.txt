The War Devil Cohabitation
On the verge of dying, Asa’s body is seized by Yoru, the War Devil, who repairs her fatal wounds in exchange for cohabitation. Sharing half of her brain with this ancient, bellicose incarnation of conflict, Asa finds her face scarred with telltale slashes whenever Yoru takes control. While Yoru possesses martial instincts and a burning desire to defeat Chainsaw Man, she remains tethered to Asa's human anxieties, creating a volatile dynamic between awkward insecurity and violent ambition.

Solitude & Murder
Asa Mitaka begins her story as a cynical, deeply alienated high school girl plagued by chronic guilt and clumsy missteps. Following the traumatic death of her mother to the Typhoon Devil, Asa isolated herself from peers, terrified of forming bonds that might end in ruin. Her fragile, solitary life comes to a sudden and violent end when a jealous class president orchestrates her murder, leaving her bleeding out on the asphalt in the dead of night.

Intercepted in the Alleyway
After a brutal, seaside clash with Denji ends in a temporary draw, Reze prepares to board a train out of Tokyo, intending to leave her mission behind. Yet, drawn by the lingering warmth of Denji's sincerity, she turns back toward the cafe where he waits with flowers. Her tragic bid for happiness is intercepted in an alleyway by Makima and the Angel Devil, extinguishing her quiet rebellion just paces away from freedom and Denji’s arms.

Rain & The Phone Booth
Reze first appears as a radiant breath of fresh air in Denji's turbulent life, sheltering him from a sudden downpour inside a quiet Tokyo telephone booth. Working part-time at a local cafe, she disarms the lonesome devil hunter with effortless charm, gentle smiles, and playful teasing. To Denji, she represents an escape from blood-soaked violence—a chance to experience innocent teenage romance, late-night swims in school pools, and a normal, peaceful life away from Public Safety.

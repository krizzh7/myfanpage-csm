The Soviet Ordnance
Beneath her gentle persona lies a terrifying reality: Reze is a Soviet military operative, raised from childhood in an underground facility as an expendable guinea pig. She is the hybrid host of the Bomb Devil, capable of pulling a grenade pin built into her neck to trigger explosive decapitation. Her severed head regenerates into a devastating combat avatar made of fuses and ordnance, turning her into a living, supersonic ballistic weapon capable of annihilating entire city blocks.

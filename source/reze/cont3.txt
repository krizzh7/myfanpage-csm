The Country Mouse
Reze’s connection with Denji is defined by Aesop’s fable of the country mouse and the city mouse—a metaphor for trading safety for luxury. While trained as an unfeeling assassin, Reze's shared moments with Denji awaken her buried desire for autonomy and genuine affection. When she urges Denji to abandon his leash and flee Tokyo with her, she is extending a desperate, sincere plea to break free from the state-sponsored violence that has defined her entire existence.
